# Matched-norm directions

Native, random and shuffled directions use shared full-model-derived input coordinates and matched per-row norms. The common mask is computed after actual float32 perturbation: all three families must have nonzero realized displacement and satisfy the recorded norm tolerance. Metrics use this intersection, not nominal direction validity. Filled circles denote full; hollow distinct markers denote inventory-effective, strictly paired ablations. The source CSV reports mean common-valid sample count across model seeds. These model-input interventions differ from raw-input corruption experiments. Points average model-seed responses; bars show model-seed SD, not independent noise replicates or confidence intervals. A smaller native-direction response is evidence consistent with directional tolerance, not proof of training-path mediation or all-direction robustness.
