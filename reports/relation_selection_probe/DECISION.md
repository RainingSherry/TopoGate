# Relation-selection probe — terminal decision

RS0, RS1, RS2, and RS3 are complete under the frozen protocol. The compact
decision is `candidate_family_problem_and_learned_rule_only_proposal`.

- RS1 found information for the label-derived pool-reference target on all
  three primary datasets, but the stricter semantic same-class target did not
  pass the full two-threshold gate.
- RS2 completed 90/90 valid selector rows. No fixed selector reached the
  primary material capture rule.
- RS3 identified material expanded-reference gaps on sms_spam_collection and
  hate_speech, with hate_speech as the extreme candidate-family sentinel. The
  gap is not isolated to hate_speech; it is a candidate-construction boundary,
  not a reason to rescue the selector route.

The current project is terminal under its authorized scope. A learned selector,
new backbone, reconstruction objective, holdout, or V-series continuation is
not authorized. Any future work must begin with a new protocol proposal and a
new pre-registration, and a separate holdout; the three primary datasets are
report-only and cannot serve as that future evaluation set. The
“learned-rule-only” wording is a scope decision, not a claim that this evidence
uniquely favors learning over candidate construction or another consumer.
