# RS3 failure map and decision

RS3 aggregates selector effects against the reused `R` reference and the
observed `H_pool` opportunity. It reports primary opportunity-development rows
separately from the Baron, Mouse, and hate_speech boundary sentinels.

The final decision is generated from the frozen pre-registration and records
whether the information bottleneck, simple-rule sufficiency, learned-rule
condition, candidate-family split, or stop boundary was reached.
