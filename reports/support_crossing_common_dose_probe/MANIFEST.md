# Publication manifest

Publish only:

- D0/D1 protocol, pre-registration and decision reports;
- compact `audit.json`, `decision.json`, `summary.json` and resolved config;
- focused source code and tests.

Do not publish:

- raw H0 or labels;
- per-row pair indices or `records.json`;
- embeddings, predictions, checkpoints, weights or logs;
- any D2 result, because D2 was not authorized.

The retained D1 status is a completed feasibility No-Go, not incomplete
compute. The range is explicitly constructive rather than exhaustive.
