# Decision boundary

M1 has only two scientific outcomes:

- `threshold_support_crossing_effect_supported`: the frozen descriptive gate
  passes; this supports a matched support-role contrast, but is not by itself a
  strict causal proof. Only then may a new protocol be proposed for M2 raw-X
  semantics.
- `support_crossing_not_isolated`: the matched control is comparable or better;
  downgrade the claim to a strong matched row-wise permutation corruption and
  stop support-specific escalation.

The additional preflight state `magnitude_match_not_estimable` means the
control cannot meet its frozen magnitude contract (it is not a negative ARI
result). In that state no M1 GPU job is authorized and the support-specific
claim remains unresolved rather than promoted.

`incomplete_compute` and `protocol_mismatch` are audit states, not negative
performance evidence. In particular, a dataset that cannot satisfy the
pre-registered magnitude tolerance is `magnitude_match_not_estimable`, not a
failed support hypothesis.

No outcome in M1 unlocks adaptive policy, GAN or a new static-library sweep.
